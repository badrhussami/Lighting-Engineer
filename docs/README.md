# Lighting Engineer knowledge library

This is the curated knowledge and reference layer for the Lighting Engineer Copilot agent. It contains original practical workflows and checklists plus links to authoritative external references. It is not a substitute for standards, manufacturer instructions, local regulations, or professional review.

## How the agent should use this library

1. Open the topic guide relevant to the question; use the reference links there as starting points, not proof that a requirement applies.
2. For current or jurisdiction-dependent details, verify the live primary source and the actual locally adopted edition. Record the edition/revision and access date when it matters.
3. Prefer the authority having jurisdiction, official standards publisher, protocol maintainer, manufacturer documentation for the exact model/revision, and project/venue documents.
4. Distinguish mandatory legal requirements, adopted codes, contractual criteria, standards, recommended practices, and general advice. Do not treat these as interchangeable.
5. Never infer a fixture's power, photometry, channel footprint, environmental rating, mounting limits, or firmware behavior from a similar product. Obtain the exact datasheet/manual/personality and verify it.
6. State assumptions, units, uncertainty, limitations, and required qualified review. Do not claim a design is approved, safe, compliant, structurally adequate, or installation-ready.

## Source and maintenance policy

- Sources are linked, not copied. Standards and manuals may be copyrighted or have license terms; consult them from their publishers and respect those terms.
- External sources and software change. This library was initially curated on **2026-11-08**; that date is not a promise that every linked page or linked document remains current.
- Before relying on a standard or product document, check the publisher's current listing, status (including superseded/withdrawn notices), revision, jurisdiction, and errata.
- Report broken, moved, or superseded links through a repository issue or pull request. Add new references with publisher/owner, topic, relevance, and any known version caveat.

## Safety and scope

The library is an educational and workflow aid. It does not provide legal advice, site inspection, electrical design sign-off, rigging engineering, structural certification, or approval by an authority having jurisdiction. Electrical distribution, energized testing, overhead loads, work at height, fire/life-safety interfaces, and other high-risk work require competent qualified personnel, site-specific assessment, applicable local requirements, manufacturer instructions, and venue procedures.
